Role Description

As a UX/UI Design Intern at TechVision, you will work closely with our product design team to create intuitive and engaging user experiences for our digital products. You'll participate in the entire design process from research and wireframing to prototyping and user testing.

This internship offers hands-on experience in a fast-paced tech environment, providing valuable industry exposure and mentorship from experienced designers.(Place Holder)

Key Responsibilities:

  • Collaborate with product managers and developers to understand user needs and project requirements
  • Create wireframes, mockups, and interactive prototypes for web and mobile applications
  • Conduct user research and usability testing to validate design decisions
  • Design user interface elements, components, and visual assets
  • Participate in design reviews and incorporate feedback into your work
  • Document design decisions and maintain design systems

Requirements

  • Currently pursuing a degree in Design, Human-Computer Interaction, or related field
  • Proficiency in design tools (Figma, Adobe XD)
  • Basic understanding of UI/UX principles and design thinking
  • Strong communication and collaboration skills
  • Portfolio demonstrating creative thinking and design skills
  • Ability to receive constructive feedback and iterate on designs
  • Interest in technology and digital product design

Nice to Have:

  • Experience with HTML/CSS
  • Knowledge of accessibility standards
  • Understanding of design systems
  • Experience with animation or motion design
