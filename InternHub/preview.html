Company Overview

ABC Corporation is a leading marketing and advertising agency based in Manila, specializing in innovative digital campaigns that help brands connect with their target audiences. With a dynamic team and a client-focused approach, we strive to deliver measurable results and creative solutions.

Role Description

As a Marketing Intern at ABC Corporation, you will support the marketing team in executing campaigns, conducting market research, and creating engaging content across various platforms.

This role is ideal for students eager to gain practical experience and develop their skills in a real-world marketing environment.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Assist in the development and execution of marketing campaigns
  • Create content for social media and promotional materials
  • Conduct market research and analyze competitor activities
  • Prepare performance reports and presentations
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams on marketing initiatives

Requirements

  • Currently pursuing a Bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Business Administration, or related field
  • Good communication and interpersonal skills
  • Proficient in Microsoft Office Suite (Excel, PowerPoint, Word)
  • Basic knowledge of social media platforms
  • Strong organizational skills and attention to detail
  • Ability to work well both independently and as part of a team
  • Available for at least 3 months of internship

Nice to Have:

  • Experience with graphic design tools like Canva or Adobe Photoshop
  • Familiarity with digital marketing analytics tools (e.g., Google Analytics)
  • Prior exposure to content creation or social media management
  • Basic understanding of SEO and online advertising
